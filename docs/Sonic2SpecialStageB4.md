# Sonic 2 Beta 4 special stage (M5): what differs from the pre-beta's, and the stages

Status 2026-11-21. The port has the **pre-beta's** stage (`docs/Sonic2SpecialStage.md`: one fixed track, no end). Beta 4's (`SpecialStage` at line 5397 of `Sonic2B4.asm`, `loc_82D4` setup) is the final game's design: seven stages, emeralds, a results screen, a way back to the level.

## Measured against the pre-beta (normalised diff of the stage's code, 2077 vs 2161 lines)
* **The track's drawing is the same code** (the unrolled decoder, about 1,150 lines, differs only by the RAM addresses: `$FFFF9Bxx` became `$FFFFDBxx`). The track blocks, tile words and curves are not separate files in Beta 4; they are in the asm (to compare when ported: the pre-beta's are already C tables).
* **The 56 track segments are byte for byte the pre-beta's** (`data/ss/0x0C8310.dat` .. `0x0D9FF4.dat` = `res/SSData/Segment00..37`), and so are the background's art, the bombs, the explosion, the emerald and the three shadows, and the two background maps (`SS_Map_0/1.eni`). So the user's "stage 4's rings are wrong" is in the order and the lists below, not in the segments.
* **What is new** (imported 2026-11-21 by `tools/import_s2b4_ss.pl`, names with the prefix `SSB4`/`B4`): Sonic and Miles in one picture (`SoncMils.nem`, 13,776 bytes, decoded to RAM by `loc_79D0`, no longer streamed by angle from many pictures), Rings, HUD font, the wings and hand, START, background stars, `PAYMW_vs`, Emeralds, the results cards (`tc_res.nem`); palettes `SS_Main` (96 bytes), `ss_1`..`ss_7` (one for each stage, palette list $1B-$21), `SS_Vs_1..3` ($22-$24), `SSResult` ($27); Kosinski data: the track's 8x8 tiles `ss_8.kos` (`SS_Tiles_8x8_Tiles`), and the three tables `ss_map_0/1/2.kos` decoded to `$FFFF6A60`, `$FFFF855C` and `$FFFF8778` (the stages' segment lists, the order of the steps and the objects' layouts), and `0x0E16AA.dat` (not read yet).
* **The PLC $3C** puts the pictures at other places ($3F40 HUD font, $4780/$4C40/$5380 shadows, $56A0 explosion, $6440 rings, $7140 START, $7BE0 PAYMW_vs, $E000 background, $EFE0 stars); $3D is only the bombs at $7140.
* **Code that is new in the main loop** (`SpecialStage`): the stage's number (`Current_Special_Stage`, `$FFFFDB94`) picks the palette ($1B+n) and the lists; an entry from the level's bonus stars (`$FFFFFFD8`, `System_Stack`) uses stages 7-9 by the emeralds' count; a stage whose emerald is held is skipped; `$FFFFDB9A` counts all the rings of the stage and `$FFFFFFA0..` (six words: the rings got per segment) is compared with it (all of them: `$FFFFFF42` the perfect flag); a pad lock `$FFFFDB87`; the end flag `$FFFFDB86`; the objects are loaded from the compact list (`$60` rings and `$61` bombs with the byte `$26`, `$59` emerald check, `$5A` rings check, and a code that starts the palette flashing `$FFFFDB95`: `loc_60D8` colours into `$FFFFFB6E` and the others cycled every 4 frames), the objects `$62` (timer) and the demo control are gone, `$87` is at `$B100`.
* **The way out** (`loc_5F40`): the stage number is kept, the perfect flag set, then (not versus) the screen is set up for the results: `Pal_MakeFlash`, palette $27, PLC list 0 and $42, the ring count and the lives kept for the card (`$FFFFF7D2/D4`), the bonus `$3E8` with the perfect flag, music $9A, the object `$6F` (the special stage results, `objects/obj_0x6F.asm`) and then the level (game mode $C); in the versus play (`$FFFFFF8A`) the results are the page 3 of `Versus_Mode_Results` (mode $18).

## Stages (each one tested; the pre-beta's stage stays until the switch)
1. **Data and art: done 2026-11-21.** `tools/import_s2b4_ss.pl`; 26 resources, listed in `paradoxmakefile.yml`.
2. **The tables: done 2026-11-21** (`SpecialStageB4Tables.{h,c}`: ss_map_1 = ten stages' step lists (kind = low 7 bits, 0-4, the pre-beta's five lists; bit 7 = the track is turned over), ss_map_2 = ten stages' object lists (pairs of a number and `$26` per step: bit 6 a bomb; the step ends on $FF, $FE = rings check object $5A, $FD = emerald check $59, $FC = palette flash; stages 7-9 are 4-6 with $FE for $FD at the end), ss_map_0 = for each of the 56 segments a count and 6-byte places of its rings and bombs (the objects read them: positions are no longer computed). Kind lengths $18 $18 $0C $10 $07 $00. Test `SpecialStageB4_TheStagesTheirObjectsAndTheSegmentsPlacesAreConsistent`. Open: `0x0E16AA.dat` (not referenced by code read so far), the ring requirements per stage (objects $59/$5A).
3. **The track with Beta 4's data:** the decoder is the same; the changes are the tile art (`ss_8.kos`), the stage's order lookup (`$FFFFDB8E`) and the palette per stage.
4. **Characters: model done 2026-11-21** (`SpecialStageB4Character.{h,c}`; Beta 4's $09/$10 are not the pre-beta's: a circle of radius $6E ($80 in front) with an angle byte and an inertia $14 (pad +-$60 to $600, let go -1/8, slope cos*$60, $1E frames of no pull), jump = velocity across the circle ($780), gravity $A8, landing where it meets the circle again, falling from the top at low speed (routine 8), bomb = tumble $20 frames, rings spilled at the first step, the other becomes the larger (`$FFFFF742`), the shadow $63 and the art pieces of the one picture `SSB4SonicMiles`: Sonic at $0/$B00/$2480/$2E20, Miles at $3060/$3800/$4C80/$53C0 from script entry $12, his tails at $55C0/$5C60/$63C0 from $24). Imported `Mappings/SSB4Sonic` and `SSData/B4CharacterArtScript`; Miles', his tails' and the shadow's mappings and the animations are the pre-beta's byte for byte. Tests `SpecialStageB4_TheCharacterRunsJumpsLandsAndTumbles` and `..._TheArtsPiecesAndTheShadowFollowTheScript`. **Not done yet:** Miles' tails object ($88), Miles' pad is the second player or the CPU (`$FFFFF606`: `loc_30E24` copies a recorded pad after $B4 frames of no input), the objects on the screen (Sst), the art's DMA. **Note:** the pre-beta disassembly folder (`~/Projects/reference/s2prebeta-disasm`) is gone, so later diffs are against the port's own C.
5. **Objects:** `$60`/`$61` with the compact list, `$59`/`$5A`, `$5E`, `$5F`, `$87`, `$63`; the palette flash at the end.
6. **The flow:** entry (the level's stars, the level select), the stage's number and emeralds, the end, the results object `$6F` and `tc_res.nem`, the way back to the level, the versus entry and results.
7. **The switch** from Sonic 1's/the pre-beta's stage, the demo and the user's play test.
