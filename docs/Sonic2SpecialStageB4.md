# Sonic 2 Beta 4 special stage (M5): what differs from the pre-beta's, and the stages

Status 2026-11-21. The port has the **pre-beta's** stage (`docs/Sonic2SpecialStage.md`: one fixed track, no end). Beta 4's (`SpecialStage` at line 5397 of `Sonic2B4.asm`, `loc_82D4` setup) is the final game's design: seven stages, emeralds, a results screen, a way back to the level.

## Measured against the pre-beta (normalised diff of the stage's code, 2077 vs 2161 lines)
* **The track's drawing is the same code** (the unrolled decoder, about 1,150 lines, differs only by the RAM addresses: `$FFFF9Bxx` became `$FFFFDBxx`). The track blocks, tile words and curves are not separate files in Beta 4; they are in the asm (to compare when ported: the pre-beta's are already C tables).
* **The 56 track segments are byte for byte the pre-beta's** (`data/ss/0x0C8310.dat` .. `0x0D9FF4.dat` = `res/SSData/Segment00..37`), and so are the background's art, the bombs, the explosion, the emerald and the three shadows, and the two background maps (`SS_Map_0/1.eni`). So the user's "stage 4's rings are wrong" is in the order and the lists below, not in the segments.
* **What is new** (imported 2026-11-21 by `tools/import_s2b4_ss.pl`, names with the prefix `SSB4`/`B4`): Sonic and Miles in one picture (`SoncMils.nem`, 13,776 bytes, decoded to RAM by `loc_79D0`, no longer streamed by angle from many pictures), Rings, HUD font, the wings and hand, START, background stars, `PAYMW_vs`, Emeralds, the results cards (`tc_res.nem`); palettes `SS_Main` (96 bytes), `ss_1`..`ss_7` (one for each stage, palette list $1B-$21), `SS_Vs_1..3` ($22-$24), `SSResult` ($27); Kosinski data: the track's 8x8 tiles `ss_8.kos` (`SS_Tiles_8x8_Tiles`), and the three tables `ss_map_0/1/2.kos` decoded to `$FFFF6A60`, `$FFFF855C` and `$FFFF8778` (the stages' segment lists, the order of the steps and the objects' layouts), and `0x0E16AA.dat` (not read yet).
* **The PLC $3C** puts the pictures at other places ($3F40 HUD font, $4780/$4C40/$5380 shadows, $56A0 explosion, $6440 rings, $7140 START, $7BE0 PAYMW_vs, $E000 background, $EFE0 stars); $3D is only the bombs at $7140.
* **Code that is new in the main loop** (`SpecialStage`): the stage's number (`Current_Special_Stage`, `$FFFFDB94`) picks the palette ($1B+n) and the lists; an entry from the level's bonus stars (`$FFFFFFD8`, `System_Stack`) uses stages 7-9 by the emeralds' count; a stage whose emerald is held is skipped; `$FFFFDB9A` counts all the rings of the stage and `$FFFFFFA0..` (six words: the rings got per segment) is compared with it (all of them: `$FFFFFF42` the perfect flag); a pad lock `$FFFFDB87`; the end flag `$FFFFDB86`; the objects are loaded from the compact list (`$60` rings and `$61` bombs with the byte `$26`, `$59` emerald check, `$5A` rings check, and a code that starts the palette flashing `$FFFFDB95`: `loc_60D8` colours into `$FFFFFB6E` and the others cycled every 4 frames), the objects `$62` (timer) and the demo control are gone, `$87` is at `$B100`.
* **The way out** (`loc_5F40`): the stage number is kept, the perfect flag set, then (not versus) the screen is set up for the results: `Pal_MakeFlash`, palette $27, PLC list 0 and $42, the ring count and the lives kept for the card (`$FFFFF7D2/D4`), the bonus `$3E8` with the perfect flag, music $9A, the object `$6F` (the special stage results, `objects/obj_0x6F.asm`) and then the level (game mode $C); in the versus play (`$FFFFFF8A`) the results are the page 3 of `Versus_Mode_Results` (mode $18).

## Stages (each one tested; the pre-beta's stage stays until the switch)

5. **Objects, rings and bombs: model done 2026-11-21** (`SpecialStageB4Items.{h,c}`: a pool of $77 items ahead of the character; `MakeStep` reads the stage's list (pairs of number and lane; bit 6 bomb; ends $FF/$FE rings check/$FD emerald check/other = palette flash) with a distance of number*4 + (the step's segments-1)*4 steps that counts down $CCCC a frame, the place on the screen from the segment's table (ss_map_0) for the lane, hidden for the lanes the table says, mirrored when the track is turned over; a ring is taken at its anim 8 by a character on the pipe within $0A of its lane (a bomb within 8, only when it is shown), the one with the smaller circle asked first; the shadow follows by the item's anim; the spill of a hit character is 10 rings off the tens (the hundreds broken into tens 9, then the ones), flying by $400/$1000 of the angle +$40+a spread, gravity $38+$80). ## Progress 2026-11-21 (the stage runs)
* **Beta 4's stage plays** (`SONIC2_START_SS=<stage 0-9>` starts in it for captures; the pre-beta's stays behind `SONIC2_SS_PREBETA`): `Object/SpecialStageB4Objects.{h,c}` makes the characters (one picture `SSB4SonicMiles`, a frame at a time to VRAM), Miles' tails, the shadows, an object for each item in the pool, the HUD, the two ring counters (the characters' and the total: multi-draw digits), the START banner and a controller (feeds the display's frame, makes a step's items, runs the items, and the end: the rings' checks and the emerald). `GM_SpecialTrack.c` runs either stage. Captured headless (frames 150, 450, 900 of stage 3): the HUD, the banner, both characters, a bomb tumble, the track turning.
* **Gotchas found:** mappings must be listed under `mappings_SonicTheHedgehog2:` in `paradoxmakefile.yml` (binary Sonic 2 format, converted by the build), not under `resources:` (they drew nothing); `TILE_MAP` does not parenthesise its arguments.
* **The rings' requirements (the user's "4th emerald is impossible"):** the stage's lists make 139, 190, 350, 141, 149, 256, 500, 149, 256 and 500 rings (stages 0-9), and the last checkpoint asks `loc_8298`'s 70, 130, 150, **170**, **190**, 190, 190, 140, 140, 140. **Stage 3 (the 4th emerald) has 141 rings for 170 and stage 4 has 149 for 190: both are impossible, as the user said** (also the first checkpoint of stage 3 asks 45 of 42 rings). Kept as in Beta 4: if a fix is wanted it goes behind `SCP_FIX_BUGS` and the choice is the user's.
* **Not done yet:** the results screen (object `$6F`) and the continue/emerald bookkeeping on the way back, the rings' gate drawn at a checkpoint (`$5A` routines 2/6: seven rings fly in, the palette flashes) and its text banners (`loc_31E14`), the emerald object drawn as it comes (`$59`), the pad lock during the end, the versus entry and results, the star post/level select entries choosing the stage (`ss4_stage` is 0 unless `SONIC2_START_SS`), the continue after a failed stage, and the switch of the other entries.

## Stage ids and progress (the user, 2026-11-21; checked against the code)
* **A stage's id is 0-6** (one player). The user thought the game does not go on without the emerald (unlike Sonic 1 and 3): **Beta 4 does that**: `DB94` (the stage number kept at the end into `Current_Special_Stage`) is increased only in the emerald's success branch (`$59`, `loc_3234C`) and the versus' one (`$5A`), so a failed stage is the same stage next time. `loc_82D4`: a number of 7 or more (the entry from the level) takes the first stage whose emerald is not won (`$FFFFFFB2+n`), and with all seven won it jumps to `EntryPoint` (the game starts again). **Stages 7-9 are the versus ones** (`System_Stack` set: number 0-2 plus 7: the `SS_Vs_1-3` palettes; their lists are the single player's stages 4-6 with the rings' check `$FE` in place of the emerald's `$FD`).
* Built: `ss4_stage` (0-6, or 7+ from the level's entry), `ss4_emerald[7]`; an emerald won marks it and goes on; with all seven won the stage sends the game to the Sega screen. Fixed the START banner's flags (Miles' tails' art entry is a single word, not a count and words: its garbage overwrote the banner's tiles).
* **The user (2026-11-21): the final version needs special stages A to D, a second page of new stages, for the versus tie breakers** (Beta 4 has three, 7-9). Keep the stage tables, palettes and the versus choice open (more than 10 stages, pages of 4).

## Failure, the checkpoint gate and the words (2026-11-21)
* **The words** (`loc_31E14`, `loc_31F48`, `loc_31ED8`; letters from the WingsHand art at tile $1A2, digits from the HUD font): after START "GET 20 RINGS !" (the number is `loc_8298`'s for the stage and the checkpoint; the digits are made from the right, 8 apart), a pass says "COOL !" and a fail "NOT ENOUGH RINGS ..." (the sentence lists `loc_31FBE`: byte = chunk number x 2, a byte of 2 starts the second line); every piece stays $3C frames and then flies away from (128,112) at 8 by its angle. After a pass the next goal's words come $3C frames later: **a pass goes on to the next goal, until the emerald or a fail** (the user asked; this is how the code is: `loc_31C7A` calls `loc_31ED8` again). A fail ends the stage after $3C + $45 frames.
* **The gate** (`$5A`): a `$FE` in the stage's list makes the check wait until the segment before the one shown is `$14` (`DB28`), then seven rings come in an arch from the middle (the 9 rows of `loc_31A8E`, one row for each segment time at `DB0D` = 4; frames 0,1,1,1,2,4,6,8,9 of the ring), the track's three lights cycle four sets of colours every 4 frames (`loc_60F8`), and at the end the sound $A1, the colours back, the rings against what is asked. The track's edge colour flashes after a `$FC` (`loc_6056`).
* **The thumbs** (the user, 2026-11-21: "this build does not use the thumbs down icon on fail yet, but directly uses the emerald fail version"): the code makes two objects (frames $14 and $15 of the letters' mappings, one y-flipped for a fail) at each result, but the user says the build shows no thumbs icon on a fail, so none is drawn here; the words are the fail. Frame $15 (4 pieces) looks like the hand. Left for the user to confirm.
* **Not done:** the emerald object drawn as it comes (`$59`'s frames of `loc_32402`), the pad lock during the end, and the results.
* **The final's two fail gates (the user, 2026-11-21):** the final game has one fail gate for failing the final (emerald) gate and a different one for an earlier checkpoint. Beta 4 has the same words for both ("NOT ENOUGH RINGS ..."; a checkpoint fail is `EndGate`, the emerald's is `RunEnd` states 2 and 4), so the two paths are already separate in the code, ready for the final's two designs.
