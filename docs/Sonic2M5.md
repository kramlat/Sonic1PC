# Sonic 2 milestone M5: Beta 4 (s2b4)

The reference is the improved disassembly of Sonic 2 Beta 4 (Esrael and Puto's, improved by Kurk and RepellantMold): https://github.com/desiresin/Sonic-2-Beta-4-Improved, kept outside this repository at `~/Projects/s2b4-disasm` (`Sonic2B4.asm`, `Constants.asm`, `macros.asm`, data in `data/`, `levels/`, `artnem/`, `sound/` ...; `S2B4_Org.bin` is the ROM). M5 follows M4 (the pre-beta, tagged `S2PB`); the censor build was skipped (the user, 2026-11-13: it was tainted with a hacktro that made its decompilation harder), so s2b4 is M5.

## Title scene and demos (the user: "title scene changes such as demo files next")

* **Demo files: nothing to change.** The five recordings in s2b4 (`Demo_Green_Hill`, `Demo_Green_Hill_Miles`, `Demo_Hill_Top`, `Demo_Casino_Night`, `Demo_Chemical_Plant`) are byte for byte the port's `res/Demo` files (NAEHZ, SWEHZTails, NAHTZ, NAHPZ, NACPZ), which are the pre-beta's. `Demo_Index` has the same entries, and the title's order (`Demo_Mode_Level_Array`: Chemical Plant $D00, Emerald Hill $000, Casino Night $C00, Hill Top $700, four used) is the pre-beta's, with the same quirks (Emerald Hill's demo turns the second-player flag on; $600 would start the special stage).
* **Title screen data: nothing to change.** Wings and background art (`titlescr.nem`), Sonic and Miles (`sncmlscr.nem`), the 1P/2P menu sprites (`1p2pVs.nem`), the three Enigma maps and the palette are the pre-beta's, byte for byte; the music is $99, the demo timer $178, the level select code the same.
* **Code differences found in the title scene** (`TitleScreen` .. `PlayLevel`), to port when M5's other parts are in: the screen starts by playing $FD (stop); it clears the two-player results table (`Results_Data_2P`, 12 words of $FFFF) on entry; the level-select flags `$FFFFFFD0` and `$FFFFFFD2` are set as words ($101 each: four flags on) where the pre-beta sets one byte; the start sound of a level, a demo and the level select is `Play_Sfx $F9` where the pre-beta plays $E0 (check the s2b4 ids: they follow its own sound table); `PlayLevel` and the special stage entry of the level select also set `$FFFFFEC6` to 3 (a second counter beside the lives: continues), clear `Current_Special_Stage` and the emerald lists (`$FFFFFFB0`, `$FFFFFFB2`, `$FFFFFFB6`) and `$FFFFFED0`/`$FFFFFED2`/`$FFFFFED6`. The level select array is the pre-beta's (Hidden Palace still disabled: the port keeps its own, as the user decided for M4).

## What differs from the pre-beta in the data (file by file, by name; `Data/` against s2b4's folders)

* **Levels:** Emerald Hill (`GHz_Map1/2.kos`), Metropolis (`Mz_Map1/3.Kos`), Casino Night (`CNz_Map1/2.kos`, its triangle lists `Tri_Act1/2.dat`, `Crawl.nem`) and Sky Fortress (`SFz_Map.Kos`) have new layouts; every zone's object list (`Obj_Act*.dat`: Emerald Hill, Hill Top, Metropolis 1-3, Oil Ocean, Dust Hill, Casino Night, Chemical Plant, Neo Green Hill, Sky Chase, Sky Fortress, Death Egg) differs; the backgrounds of Hill Top and Chemical Plant (`Backgnd.dat`).
* **Palettes:** Sonic's (`sonic.pal`), Casino Night, Chemical Plant (and its underwater), Dust Hill, Hidden Palace and Neo Green Hill underwater, and the special stage's (`SS_Main.pal`).
* **Art:** `Rings`, `Monitors`, `EndPanel`, `Robotnik` (the ship), `WatrSurf`, Oil Ocean's `Oil_01`; the special stage's `Emerald`, `HUD_Font`, `Rings` and `WingHand`.
* **Sound:** every effect (76 files: the final game's are already in), the 1-up, game over, emerald and credits music, and DAC $03.
* The same-named files that are identical are not listed. Files only in s2b4 by name are mostly renames (`artnem/cnzBoss.nem` for `Data/CNz/Boss.nem` and so on): compare them by content when each area is done.
